SATIE-1 — Erik. Evolved.
By OCKM Studio
=======================
I've kept it simple. Choose a tune and a mood, then let the music evolve while
you work. You can stay on Home: four knobs are enough to make it yours.

FIRST MINUTE
1. Turn MASTER down before you start, then bring it up comfortably.
2. PRESET chooses a tune. SELECT chooses a mood.
3. Press HOME. The four knobs are Tempo, Energy, Evolve and Mix.
4. Leave it running, or press REC to hold an eight-bar phrase you like.

HOME MIX: SOLO / DUET / TRIO
0 = lead and drums; 45 = add bass and atmosphere; 85 = add the third voice.
Mix blends between these arrangements. Delay's Wet control is separate.

TUNES
Press SEL to browse Gymnopedies, Gnossiennes and Ogives. Turn SELECT or PRESET,
press SEL to choose. HOME goes back. Confirmed changes begin on the next bar.

MAKE IT YOURS
Turn ALGORITHM through Home, Lead, Bass, Drums, Layers, Colour, Delay and Space.
The four knobs follow the labels on the screen. Instrument Level 50 keeps the
mood's balance; 0 mutes that part. EDIT resets only the page you are editing.
Colour offers Clean / Vinyl / Tape / Grit, amount, wobble and dust.
Delay offers time / repeats / tone / wet. Space shapes reverb and stereo width.
Ibiza Chill now has a steady background: no automatic chorus or pumping.
Choose Vinyl or Tape and raise Wobble when you want deliberate pitch movement.

HOW EVOLVE WORKS
The stored score continues while its treatment changes every eight four-beat
bars (32 seconds at 60 BPM). The new treatment comes in over one bar. Evolve
controls how often eligible lead notes are changed, not the section length.
At zero, the lead follows the source notes. Higher values bring more octave,
density and timing changes. These are programmed treatments of the original
score, not new compositions generated from the previous variation.
The score loops at its end: Gymnopedie 1 lasts about 2:24 at its default 60 BPM.

KEEP, RECALL, HELP
SAVE keeps one favourite, including every page. GLO opens the guide.
Turn SELECT or ALGORITHM to read it; SEL advances a page.
On the final Keep and Recall page, SEL recalls your saved track.
HOME or GLO closes the guide. Music continues while you read.

HOME then EDIT makes a fresh rhythm/variation seed on the next bar.
PLAY pauses/resumes. REC holds/releases the current eight-bar passage.
The small Erik portrait appears at startup; a button skips it.

LIBRARY AND MOODS
Gymnopedies 1–3; Gnossiennes 1–3; Ogives 1–4.
After Hours / Ibiza Chill / Orbit / Blue Note / Ghost.
Simplified electronic score arrangements, not recordings or literal piano
performances. The companion SOURCES.txt gives edition credits and licences.
