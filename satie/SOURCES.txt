SATIE-1 SOURCE NOTES
====================
Erik Satie. Ten complete exported scores, reduced to lead, bass and inner voice.
The original MIDI/LilyPond editions are retained in sources/. source-notes.json
records their SHA-256 hashes and all reduced notes. No recordings are embedded.

Reduction: highest new right-staff note per sixteenth; lowest active left-staff
note each quarter; second-highest active pitch as an optional inner voice.
The original quarter-note timing is retained, rounded to sixteenths and padded
to 4/4 bars. This simplifies durations, chords, ornaments and source metre.
Evolve makes optional register/rhythmic changes. Hold loops eight dance bars.

Gymnopedie editions: public domain, engraved by Evin Robertson.
Gnossienne editions/adapted note data: CC BY-SA 4.0, original notices retained.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Ogive MIDI editions: David Cooke, explicitly released into the public domain
for copying and adaptation. Source pages and original URLs are in the matching
sources/ogive_N.json files and below. The sequencer uses right and left staves
on channels 0 and 1; embedded tempo changes are replaced by the chosen tempo.
