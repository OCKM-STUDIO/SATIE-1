Satie, with room to breatheThree Gymnopédies, three Gnossiennes and four Ogives. Each piece follows a complete score reduction while its electronic arrangement gently evolves. At zero Evolve, the lead follows the reduced source pitches. Hold repeats the current eight-bar passage. Browse by collection with SEL, or turn PRESET to choose a tune.
One dial, an evolving arrangementMix starts with lead and drums, gradually adds bass and atmosphere, then brings in an inner score voice or a quiet melodic answer. Turn further for a fuller, more spacious blend. Orbit floats; Blue Note pairs piano and upright bass with soft brushes; Ghost adds dark texture and a broken beat. After Hours and Ibiza Chill offer more rhythmic alternatives.
Shape each instrumentUse Algorithm to reach Lead, Bass, Drums and Layers. Adjust the sound while the score keeps moving, or reset any page with EDIT.
Colour and echoesVinyl adds sampler-inspired compression and warmth, with independent wobble and dust. Tape and Grit offer other textures. Delay follows the tempo; Space shapes the room and stereo width.
